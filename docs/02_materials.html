2. Materials

One material model covers the whole wurtzite AlxInyGa1−x−yN system. The classes AlGaN, InGaN and InAlGaN are views of the same function, get_nitride_params(x_Al, x_In), so any two layers are always described by mutually consistent parameters.

#2.10 Temperature dependence

Three quantities depend on the simulation temperature : the thermal energy in the carrier statistics, the spontaneous polarization (4.3), and the band gap. The gap follows the Varshni relation, written as a shift from its 300 K value so that all 300 K parameters above are unchanged:

For an alloy the shift is interpolated linearly (2.1) between the binaries.

GaN AlN InN Source
(meV/K) 0.909 1.799 0.245 Vurgaftman & Meyer 2003
(K) 830 1462 624 Vurgaftman & Meyer 2003
(meV) 72 92 24 from (2.10)
at 10 K (eV) 3.462 6.118 0.714 from (2.10)

The whole shift is applied to the conduction band: the valence-band offsets (2.4) are kept independent of temperature.

ImportantEffective masses, dielectric constants, lattice constants and dopant ionization energies keep their 300 K values at every temperature. The gap is the band-to-band gap; exciton binding (about 25 meV in GaN, 80 meV in AlN) is not subtracted, so a low-temperature excitonic line lies below by that amount.
