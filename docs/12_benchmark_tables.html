A. Benchmark tables

Every paper of the literature benchmark of chapter 10: the structure or observable, the measured value, the value computed by the tool with default settings, the difference and the score. Where a row lists several values they are separated by slashes, in the same order in every column. Densities are in cm−2 or cm−3, energies in eV, fields in MV/cm, thicknesses in nm.

Confidence refers to the source, not to the tool: High means structure and value are stated in the paper; Medium means a dimension or doping level was assumed, or the value was read from a figure or abstract; Low means a major assumption.

#Notes on individual rows

  • Yim et al., JAP 44, 292 (1973): early absorption edge; modern RT value ~6.0-6.1 eV
  • Monemar, PRB 10, 676 (1974): tests temperature dependence of Eg
  • Khan et al., APL 60, 3027 (1992): barrier thickness/polarity not public: assumed 30 nm Ga-face Al0.13GaN on GaN
  • Goetz et al., APL 68, 3144 (1996): Ed 12-17 meV; 15 meV used
  • Martin et al., APL 68, 2541 (1996): InN/GaN 1.05+-0.25, GaN/AlN 0.70+-0.24, InN/AlN 1.81+-0.20 eV
  • Schmitz et al., Semicond. Sci. Technol. 11, 1464 (1996): mean of the I-V and C-V values; tool = Schottky-Mott (no pinning)
  • Brunner et al., JAP 82, 5090 (1997): compared as Eg(x=0.5) shift implied by bowing b: tool 0.7 eV vs measured 1.3+/-0.2 eV
  • Li, Lin, Jiang, Khan & Chen, JVST B 15, 1117 (1997): measured at 10 K (dark, read from Fig. 4); simulated at 77 K
  • Sun et al., APL 70, 2978 (1997): from stimulated-emission shifts: dEc 130-155 meV, dEv 245-220 meV (strained wells)
  • Takeuchi et al., JJAP 36, L382 (1997): 1.08 MV/cm (piezo field consistent with their PL well-width series); 3 nm QW in thick GaN assumed
  • Kim et al., APL 73, 3426 (1998): lower limit 560 kV/cm
  • Leroux et al., PRB 58, R13371 (1998): 450 kV/cm; single well between thick barriers assumed
  • Ambacher et al., JAP 85, 3222 (1999): barriers 20-65 nm in paper; 30 nm assumed
  • Grandjean et al., JAP 86, 3714 (1999): ~1 MV/cm reported at x=0.27; single 3 nm QW between thick barriers assumed
  • Cai et al. (McCartney, Ponce), MRS Proc. 639 (2000): 2.2+-0.6 MV/cm
  • Ibbetson et al., APL 77, 250 (2000): surface donors at 1.65 eV (paper); onset defined as n_s > 1e11 cm^-2
  • Kaufmann et al., PRB 62, 10867 (2000): p_max ~6e17 at [Mg]~2e19; tool has no self-compensation model
  • Qiao et al., JAP 87, 801 (2000): internal photoemission: 1.25 eV (x=0.15), 1.29 eV (x=0.23); table columns read from the PDF text
  • Hang et al., JAP 90, 1887 (2001): dEc:dEv = 65:35; scored as dEc at x = 0.2 with the tool gap difference
  • Jho et al., PRB 64, 035334 (2001): 2.1+-0.2 MV/cm; 3 nm well assumed
  • Nam et al., APL 78, 3690 (2001): n=1 interband transition 4.039 eV; 5-period MQW on AlN simulated at 300 K, V = 0
  • Davydov et al., pss(b) 229, R1 (2002): PL band 0.75-0.85 eV close to the absorption edge; 0.80 used
  • Jena et al., APL 81, 4395 (2002): 300 K Hall, Table I; surface at graded-layer top (Ambacher barrier)
  • Li et al., APL 80, 1210 (2002): Ea = 0.262 / 0.279 / 0.311 eV at x = 0.22 / 0.25 / 0.27
  • Miller et al., APL 80, 3551 (2002): sigma = 2.36 / 6.79 / 6.92 e12 cm^-2 at x = 0.05 / 0.12 / 0.16
  • Wu et al., APL 80, 3967 (2002): paper: gap between 0.7 and 0.8 eV; mid value used
