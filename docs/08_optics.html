#8.5 Relation to measured spectra

The reported is not a prediction of a luminescence peak. The difference is made up of effects outside the model:

Effect Typical size Shifts measurement
exciton binding 20-30 meV in GaN, up to 80 meV in AlN, larger in narrow wells down
temperature (gap fixed at its 300 K value) 60-110 meV between 300 K and 10 K up at low
screening of the field by injected carriers up to several 100 meV in Al-rich wells up
localisation in alloy and width fluctuations 20-100 meV down
Stokes shift 0-100 meV down, emission only

For a wide polar well at zero bias can be below 1%. A device does not emit from that state; by the time it emits, the injected carriers have partly screened the field. The comparison with electroluminescence should then be made with a biased solution.
