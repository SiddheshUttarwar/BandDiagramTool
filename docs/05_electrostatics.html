#5.8 The surface barrier and thin barriers

For a barrier of thickness , (5.19) gives the sensitivity of the sheet density to the surface barrier:

Barrier
AlN 2 nm cm−2/eV
AlN 4 nm cm−2/eV
Al0.3Ga0.7N 25 nm cm−2/eV

For a 25 nm AlGaN barrier an error of 0.3 eV in changes by about 6%. For 2 nm of AlN the same error changes it by cm−2, which is more than the measured density of such a sample. The absolute density under a thin barrier cannot be predicted without knowing the surface potential of that sample.

The barrier of a free nitride surface is not a constant either. Li, Cao, Xing and Jena (Appl. Phys. Lett. 97, 222110, 2010) found that no fixed describes Al0.72Ga0.28N/GaN over a range of thickness: the barrier they extract falls from 3.5 to 2.2 eV as the 2DEG density rises.

Inverse problem. The meaningful comparison for thin barriers is therefore the barrier that reproduces a measured density:

from devices.analysis import extract_surface_barrier
phi_B = extract_surface_barrier(layers, n_s_measured, window_nm=(z0, z1))

It solves by bracketing and Brent's method, each evaluation being a full solve of (5.1). Results for published thin-barrier samples are in section 10.5.

WarningAny fixed-barrier Schrödinger-Poisson calculation, with this tool or another, will overestimate or underestimate thin-barrier densities by a large factor unless is taken from the sample. Treat a forward prediction for barriers below about 4 nm as a function of an unknown , not as a number.
