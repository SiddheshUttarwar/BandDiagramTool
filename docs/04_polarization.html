4. Polarization

Wurtzite nitrides carry a built-in electric polarization along the c-axis. Its discontinuities at heterointerfaces are fixed charges of order cm−2, which is what creates two-dimensional electron and hole gases in undoped structures and tilts every quantum well.

#4.8 Alternative parameter set: Dreyer et al. 2016

The default constants (sections 4.1 and 4.2) follow Ambacher (2002) and Bernardini (1997). Dreyer, Janotti, Van de Walle and Vanderbilt (Phys. Rev. X 6, 021038) showed that the zincblende reference used for those spontaneous-polarization values is not a consistent zero between different materials, and that interface charges require the improper piezoelectric constant

#4.9 Scope

  • c-plane only, either polarity. Semi-polar and non-polar orientations are not available.
  • Linear piezoelectricity; no second-order terms.
  • No screening of by interface traps unless a SurfaceCharge is added.
