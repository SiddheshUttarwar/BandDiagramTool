14. Defects under illumination

Light that a doped layer absorbs while it grows creates excess minority carriers. A charged compensating defect then no longer forms against the equilibrium Fermi level, its formation energy rises, and fewer of it are incorporated. The method was developed for MOCVD of GaN and AlGaN at North Carolina State University under the name defect quasi-Fermi level control.

This chapter states the estimate the tool makes of that effect. It is available from the command line only (examples/06_defects_under_illumination.py); the implementation is physics/dqfl.py.

WarningThis is an estimate, not a validated prediction. Section 14.8 compares it with three published data sets: it is within a factor of two to eight of them, and one result depends on a parameter that is not known.
